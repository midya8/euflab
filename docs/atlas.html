A small universe. An exact boundary.
At most eight subterm-closed ground terms, one uninterpreted sort and conjunctions only. No quantified constraints, arithmetic, SMT-LIB parser or full-interpretation counting. Exhaustive enumeration is deliberately exponential. Native and browser implementations use separate partition enumerators. Downloaded reports can be independently checked with the native CLI; no verification server or model service is contacted.
