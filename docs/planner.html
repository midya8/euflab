02 / AUDIT THE DECISION

Protected-core obstruction
A core entirely inside the protected set cannot be hit by a permitted deletion. All such cores are listed. Otherwise at least one plan exists, even if it removes every unprotected occurrence.
All optima, or a different objective?
Cost-only retains every cheapest deletion set, even a free redundant deletion. The Pareto view minimizes cost and count together: a plan dominates another only if neither coordinate is worse and at least one is better. Equal coordinates retain every tied plan. Neither view silently replaces the other.
